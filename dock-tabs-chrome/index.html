Tab magnification lands in Chrome

Sixty tabs, and not a favicon squeezed out of existence. Sweep the mouse across the tab strip: only the icons grow, up to 4× — biggest under the pointer, tapering off to each side — and the name of the one icon you are actually on appears below it. Tick Vertical tabs above the window to swap the strip for the side list, where there is room for names, so rows keep their titles inline and widen to fit. Either way, the wheel scrolls the tabs that run off the end.

Hover the tabs to magnify them
